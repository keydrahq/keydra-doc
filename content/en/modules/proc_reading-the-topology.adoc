= Reading the topology

.Prerequisites

* You hold `connection:view` on the target.

.Procedure

. Open the target and go to *Topology*.
. For a cluster, select a shard or a node in the graph to see its detail: *Link*, *Flags*,
  slot ranges and node ID.

.Verification

The slot bar accounts for every slot. Hovering a range says which address serves it and how
many slots that is.
