= A target will not connect

The row stays *Down*, or *Test connection* fails.

.Procedure

. Check that {product-name} can reach the address at all. It is {product-name} that
  connects, not your browser — an address that works from your laptop may not work from the
  container.
. If the target is on a private network, give the profile a tunnel — see
  xref:tunnels.adoc[Reaching targets through a jump host].
. Check the credentials. A target that requires a password and is given none refuses at
  authentication rather than at connection, which looks the same from the outside.
. If the target speaks TLS, tick *Use TLS*. If it presents a certificate signed by a private
  authority, that authority has to be in the truststore of the machine {product-name} runs
  on — {product-name} trusts the system store and has no setting of its own.
. For a Sentinel profile, check the *Sentinel master name*. {product-name} asks Sentinel
  where that master is; a name Sentinel does not know produces no address.

.Verification

*Test connection* reports success against what the form currently says, without saving
anything.
