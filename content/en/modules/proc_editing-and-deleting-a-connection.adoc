= Editing and deleting a connection

.Prerequisites

* You hold `connection:edit` on the target to change it, or `connection:delete` to remove
  it.

.Procedure

To change a profile:

. On *Connections*, open the row's actions menu and choose *Edit*.
. Change what needs changing. The password field arrives empty and marked *Stored — leave
  empty to keep it*: leaving it alone keeps the stored password, and typing replaces it.
. Click *Save*.

To remove one:

. Open the row's actions menu and choose *Delete*.
. The dialog reads *Delete connection?* and states that data on the target server is not
  affected.
. Confirm.

.Verification

An edit closes the profile's pooled connections and its tunnel, because the profile may now
point somewhere else; the status returns to *Checking…* and then to whatever the new
address answers.

A deleted profile disappears from every list, including the fleet overview and the target
list a migration offers. Nothing on the server itself changes.
