= Analysing a keyspace

.Prerequisites

* You hold `analysis:read` on the target.

.Procedure

. Open the target and go to *Analysis*.
. Read the panels: *Memory by type*, *When this memory comes back*, *Memory by namespace*,
  and *Largest keys*.
. Click *Measure again* after a change, to see whether it did what you expected.

.Verification

The header states whether every key was measured or a sample was, and how large that sample
was. An empty database says *Nothing to measure* rather than drawing empty charts.
