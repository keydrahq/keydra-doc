= Exporting and importing keys

.Prerequisites

* `transfer:export` to export, `transfer:import` to import.

.Procedure

To export:

. Open the target's *Keys* page.
. Either select rows and use *Export {count} keys*, or use *Export everything matching* to
  export what the current glob matches.
. The file downloads.

To import:

. On the *Keys* page, open *Import and export* and choose the import action.
. Choose the file.
. Decide what happens to a key that already exists: replace it, or leave it alone.
. Confirm.

.Verification

The key list updates as keys arrive — the change goes out over the notification hub, so the
count moves without a rescan.
