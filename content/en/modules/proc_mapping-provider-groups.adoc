= Mapping provider groups to Keydra groups

.Prerequisites

* The provider sends a groups claim, and *Groups claim* names it.

.Procedure

. Open the provider and find *Group mappings*.
. Add a mapping: *What the provider calls it*, and the {product-name} group somebody in it
  should join.

.Verification

Sign in through the provider. The account's memberships in mapped groups now match what the
provider said.

[IMPORTANT]
====
Membership of a *mapped* group is replaced at every sign-in. Adding somebody to a mapped
group by hand in {product-name} will not survive their next sign-in — that is the point, and
it is what makes removing them from the directory remove their access here.

Groups nobody mapped are left alone.
====
