= The audit log

Every operation that changes something is recorded: who did it, what, to which target, when,
and whether it succeeded or was refused.

Reads are not recorded. A log of every page view buries the entries somebody will come
looking for.

.Procedure

. Go to *Audit*. You need `audit:read`.
. Filter by *What* and by *Who*, and page through.

image::audit-log.jpg["The audit log, with what was done, by whom, on which target, and whether it was done or refused"]

.Verification

Each row names the subject of the change in the interface's own words — *Connection*,
*Grant*, *Encryption key*, *Backup destination*, *Schedule* and so on — with the outcome
*done* or *refused*.

A refused operation is recorded too. An attempt somebody was not allowed to make is exactly
the entry an audit log exists for.
