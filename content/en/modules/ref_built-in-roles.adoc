= The built-in roles

include::../snippets/generated/roles-table.adoc[]

These three cannot be edited. A deployment that needs something between them defines a role
of its own, on the *Roles* tab of the *Access* page, choosing permissions from
xref:permissions-reference.adoc[the full list].

.Existing role claims keep working
[NOTE]
====
A token carrying a `viewer`, `operator` or `admin` claim becomes a grant of that role on the
instance scope. A deployment that was using the three global roles keeps working before
anybody configures anything.
====
